Mission

Autonomous construction machinery needs real data.
We provide it from active European job sites.

The construction and earthmoving sector is entering its autonomy era. But the AI models powering autonomous excavators and heavy machinery are starved of one critical resource: real-world training data with reliable ground truth from machines actually working on real sites — in European conditions.

Pigna AI solves this. Through a strategic partnership with an operating Italian construction company, we instrument entire fleets with industrial sensors and synchronized cameras — producing excavator datasets and heavy equipment data where every frame is mathematically annotated, not by humans.

Our fleet includes Volvo, Caterpillar, Hitachi, Doosan and New Holland excavators operating on active construction sites in Southern Italy. Real soil conditions. Real operating cycles. Real ground truth.

Exclusive fleet access — Italy

Through our partnership with an established Southern Italian construction company, Pigna AI has exclusive instrumentation rights across a diverse operational fleet — actively working on real earthmoving and construction sites every day.

Phase 1 covers 11 tracked excavators across 6 major OEM brands: Volvo, Caterpillar, Hitachi, Doosan, New Holland and Komatsu. Each machine is a unique data source — different weight class, hydraulic configuration, attachment type and operating environment.

About

Pigna AI is an Italian SRLS building the data infrastructure for industrial autonomy and physical AI. We identified the same bottleneck everywhere: autonomous construction machinery and earthmoving AI are limited not by algorithms or compute, but by the absence of real-world excavator data collected in European operating conditions.

Through our exclusive partnership with an operating construction company — active since 1966, ISO certified, operating across 8 Italian regions — we have ground-truth access to one of Italy's most diverse heavy-machinery fleets. We are starting with excavator CAN bus data and multi-camera sensor fusion. We are not stopping there.

Founded by computer engineers and civil engineers. Based in Lacedonia (AV), Italy. Member of the NVIDIA Inception Program.
