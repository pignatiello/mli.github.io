Mission

Autonomous machinery needs real data.
We provide it at industrial scale.

The construction, mining, and heavy industry sectors are entering their autonomy era. But the AI models that will power this transition are starved of one thing: real-world training data with reliable ground truth from machines actually working on real sites.

Pigna AI solves this. Through a strategic partnership with an operating construction company, we instrument entire fleets with industrial sensors and synchronized cameras, producing massive datasets where every frame is mathematically annotated — by physics, not by humans.

About

Pigna AI is an Italian SRLS building the data infrastructure that powers industrial autonomy. We saw the same pattern everywhere: physical AI is bottlenecked not by algorithms or compute, but by real-world data.

So we built the company that solves it. Through our exclusive partnership with an operating construction company, we have ground-truth access to one of Italy's most diverse heavy-machinery fleets. We're starting with excavators. We're not stopping there.

Founded by a team of computer engineers and civil engineers, Pigna AI is based in Lacedonia (AV), Italy.
